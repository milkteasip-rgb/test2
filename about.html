About

I work on the part where a brand meets a product.

I studied graphic design in Seoul and spent four years in branding there before moving to New York for product work. Those two halves are the job now: an identity that only exists as a PDF is unfinished, and a product with no visual argument is forgettable.

Independent since 2024. I take a small number of projects a year, usually six to twelve weeks, and I prefer to stay through build rather than hand over a file.

On Sundays I draw a text face called Yi Text. Two of six weights exist.
